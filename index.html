Candidate Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers to Help You Move Forward.

Whether you're exploring your next career transition or scaling a capable team, here are straightforward answers to our most common inquiries.

Have a specific question not covered here?

Our placement specialists are ready to discuss your unique hiring or career situation directly.

Direct response within 2 business hours
01 How does Future Nest help candidates?

We work with candidates to understand their career goals, experience, and what they're looking for in a role. From there, we help strengthen their resume and profile, connect them with relevant opportunities, prepare them for interviews, and support them through the offer and onboarding stages. Every candidate's journey is different, and we tailor our support accordingly.

Candidate Guidance 1:1 Coaching
02 What types of roles do you support?

We support placement across technology, finance, healthcare, engineering, marketing, operations, retail, and professional services. Roles range from entry-level to senior positions across permanent, contract, and hybrid arrangements. If you're unsure whether your role or background is a fit, contact us directly and we'll be honest with you about what we can offer.

Industry Coverage All Career Levels
03 Do you work with employers directly?

Yes. We work directly with hiring managers and people teams to understand role requirements, team structure, culture, and timeline. We handle sourcing, screening, and candidate presentation — and we coordinate communication throughout the process. Employers can engage us for individual roles or broader recruitment support.

Employer Solutions Direct Hiring
04 How does the recruitment process work?

For candidates, the process typically moves through: initial conversation → profile and resume review → opportunity matching → interview preparation → interviews → offer stage. For employers, it follows: requirements briefing → candidate sourcing → screening and shortlisting → coordinated interviews → offer and close. We keep both sides informed at each step.

Structured Workflow Clear Milestones
05 How long does placement typically take?

Every hiring process is different. Timelines depend on the role type, the employer's internal process, and the candidate's availability and readiness. We cannot predict or guarantee specific timeframes. What we can do is keep the process organised and communication consistent, which typically reduces unnecessary delays on both sides.

Realistic Timelines Active Updates
06 Do you support remote opportunities?

Yes. We work with candidates and employers across on-site, hybrid, and fully remote arrangements. The availability of remote options varies by role, industry, and employer. We're transparent about what each opportunity involves so candidates can make informed decisions before investing time in the process.

Remote / Hybrid Workplace Flexibility
07 Can employers request multiple candidates?

Yes. We can support single-role hiring or broader talent needs across multiple positions or functions. For organisations with ongoing hiring requirements, we can discuss an arrangement that reflects the scope and continuity of the work involved.

Scale & Volume Team Building
08 How do I get started?

Use the contact form on this page or reach us by email. Tell us briefly whether you're a candidate or an employer, and what you're looking for. We'll respond to arrange an initial conversation where we can understand your situation properly before moving forward.

Fast Setup No Obligation
