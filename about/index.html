Why FUTURENEST Was Created

FutureNest Placements was founded in 2023 after recognizing a recurring challenge: talented international graduates with strong qualifications and real skills were often being filtered out by Applicant Tracking Systems before their profiles ever reached a recruiter.

  • Weak resume positioning
  • Missing relevant keywords
  • Unclear professional branding
  • Low ATS visibility
  • Limited recruiter reach
  • Missed job opportunities

FutureNest was built to help close that gap

Frequently asked
QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our job placement services is covered in these FAQs. If you’re looking for something else, contact us.

How do international students get a job in the USA?

A focused search starts with clearly presenting your skills, tailoring your resume to relevant roles, and preparing for interviews. FutureNest helps with profile positioning and job-search preparation. Your university’s international student office can help with questions about your individual work authorization.

Which is the best agency for IT and Non IT job placement service for international students and job seekers?

Look for an agency that explains its services, fees, and process clearly and takes time to understand your experience and goals. Book a consultation with FutureNest to discuss whether our support fits your needs.

Do you offer job placement for OPT and CPT students?

FutureNest supports the job-search preparation of international students and professionals on OPT, STEM OPT, and CPT. Contact our team to discuss your background and the support available. Confirm authorization requirements with your university before accepting work.

Can international students get H1B visa sponsorship jobs in the USA?

Sponsorship decisions are made by individual employers. FutureNest can help you prepare your profile and discuss your search goals, but cannot promise sponsorship or a visa outcome. Ask prospective employers about their sponsorship policies.

Do you provide full-time jobs or contract jobs?

Our focus is helping candidates pursue meaningful full-time roles. Contact our team to discuss your preferences and the opportunities relevant to your experience.

How much does the job placement service of Futurenest cost?

Fees depend on the support you need. Book a free consultation to discuss the services, pricing, and agreement before deciding whether to proceed.

Why should companies use a staffing agency instead of hiring in-house?

A recruitment partner can support sourcing, screening, and interview coordination, giving an internal team more time to focus on its business. The right approach depends on the role, hiring volume, and resources available.

Can companies outsource talent acquisition to external partners?

Yes. An external recruitment partner can support selected stages of hiring or a broader search process. Contact FutureNest to discuss your roles, timeline, and the level of support your team needs.

Can one company handle recruitment, payroll, and compliance together?

FutureNest offers recruitment, background verification, and payroll and tax support. Speak with our team to confirm which services and responsibilities would be included in your agreement.

Where is Futurenest based?

FutureNest Placements is based in Wilmington, Delaware, and supports candidates and employers across the United States. Visit our contact page to get in touch.
